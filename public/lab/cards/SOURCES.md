# Lab Draw card images: sources

The 22 major arcana from the original 1909 Rider-Waite-Smith deck, drawn by
Pamela Colman Smith (1878–1951), first published by William Rider & Son, 1909
(the "Roses & Lilies" scans on Wikimedia Commons).

**Licence: public domain.** Published before 1929 (public domain in the US), and the
artist died in 1951, so the work is out of copyright in the UK and EU (life + 70
years, from 2022). The later recoloured editions (U.S. Games, 1971 onwards) are a
different work and are NOT used here. The five `public/images/The_*.JPG` files used
by the services page are a separate set and were not checked.

Processing: downloaded 2026-11-06 from upload.wikimedia.org, resized to 240 px and
480 px wide, saved as WebP at quality 78. No other changes.
